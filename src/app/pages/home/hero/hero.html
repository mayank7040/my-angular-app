Welcome to AMS

(Ahmedabad Montessori School)

At AMS, we believe in nurturing young minds through the Montessori philosophy – encouraging independence, creativity, and tactile discovery. Our learning environment is thoughtfully designed for children aged 2 to 4 to explore through touch, movement, and play. From real-world materials to joyful interactions, we create a space where curiosity is celebrated and every child builds a strong foundation for lifelong learning.
